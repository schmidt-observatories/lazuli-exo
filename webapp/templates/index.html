Feature S/N

FeatureS/N
Matched-template amplitude S/N against the best-fit flat line (diagonal errors, no nuisance terms) — not a calibrated detection significance. Per-band values restrict the same statistic to fixed wavelength masks; they mix every species in the mask plus haze/cloud/continuum structure (H2O and CH4 overlap near 1.15 µm) and are not molecule detections. CO and NH3 have no masks in 0.4–1.7 µm and are never reported.
